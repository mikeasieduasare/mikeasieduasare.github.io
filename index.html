01

Research

My work focuses on the technical foundations required to make AI systems reliable enough to operate in environments where failure matters.

01

Robustness & Reliability

Understanding how intelligent systems behave under distribution shift, perturbation, uncertainty, and changing operating conditions.

02

Verification & Formal Methods

Developing methods for reasoning about system behavior, failure modes, and guarantees before deployment.

03

Interpretability & Alignment

Investigating how internal representations and model behavior can provide evidence about what systems are doing and why.

04

Safe Deployment

Designing systems that monitor their own operating conditions and respond appropriately when confidence or reliability degrades.
